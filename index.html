Schreiben: the check runs on this device. It lines up her strokes with the printed letter, then checks that every part of the letter is there, that the dots are complete and in the right place (a dash or a “^” for two or three dots is fine), and that there are no extra lines. If her letter looks more like another letter, the owl says which one. It does not check stroke order or direction. If you think she wrote it correctly, use the ✔ button after two tries.

Speech recognition checks that the right word was said, but it can't hear vowel marks (tashkeel) precisely. Listen along and use the ✔ button if you disagree. Works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari, with the microphone allowed.

Sprechen: the phone only understands words, it cannot hear how the “s” or “z” is made. With “Parents”, you rate the sound yourself (hold 😀 / 🙂 / 😕 for a moment) and your rating counts as the score. With “Phone only”, the score just shows how clearly the phone understood the word. The Zisch-Test measures the hiss directly from the microphone; it is experimental and works best after calibrating it once (buttons on that page). None of this is a diagnosis – a speech therapist (Logopäde) can rate her lisp properly.
